# Deal rules

These rules decide which Village Grocer posts count as an item deal.

## Keep a post when

It advertises a price deal on specific food or grocery items. Any of these counts:

- Buy 1 Free 1 (B1F1), Buy 2 at RMx, 2nd item % off
- A % discount or a special or promo price on named items
- Weekly specials, weekend specials, "Fresh deals" lists
- Anything called "Bumper" or "Bumper Harvest"

Item types in scope:

- Fruit and vegetables
- Bread, bakery and pastries
- Chicken, pork, beef, lamb, seafood and fish
- Eggs, dairy, butter and cheese
- Rice, noodles, cooking oil and pantry staples
- Jam, spreads and condiments
- Drinks: water, juice, soft drinks, coffee and tea

## Skip a post when

- It promotes a new store, outlet or grand opening, even if it lists item deals or calls itself a "Bumper Bonanza" for the opening.
- It is a product showcase, recipe or seasonal pre-order with no price deal.
- The only offer is a delivery or online promo code.
- It is a spend-based voucher or minimum-spend code (e.g. "RM10 off with min. spend RM250, code FD10").
- It needs a spend threshold to get a gift, game or redemption (e.g. "spend RM35 on Brand X, get a free tote").
- It is an app, membership or loyalty points promo.
- It is a contest or giveaway.
- It is about non-food items (kitchenware, household and personal care).

## Examples

| Post | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| "Emborg butter RM14.49, Basmathi rice 2kg RM19.99, valid 13–16 Nov" | Keep |
| "Bakery: 60% off your 2nd item, 16–30 Nov" | Keep |
| "Frozen Dragon Tiger Grouper RM55.99/pc" | Keep |
| "Spend RM25 on Puck or Darbo and get a free gift" | Skip (spend-based) |
| "Pre-order Christmas set meals for pickup" | Skip (no deal) |
| "Grand opening at our new outlet, Bumper Bonanza deals!" | Skip (store opening) |
| "Use code FD10 for RM10 off min. spend RM250" | Skip (min-spend code) |
| "Double points for members this weekend" | Skip (loyalty points) |
